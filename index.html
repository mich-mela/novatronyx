PROGRAM OVERVIEW · NASA JOHNSON SPACE CENTER

About the NovaTronyx Astronaut Mission Trainer Program

Grounded 100% in NASA's official Astronaut Candidate (ASCAN) two-year basic training syllabus conducted at the Lyndon B. Johnson Space Center (JSC) in Houston, Texas.

NASA ASCAN FOUNDATION

Adult Astronaut Candidate Concept

Since NASA's Original Mercury Seven (1959) through the 2021 "The Flies" (Group 23) and 2025 Astronaut Candidate Class, selected candidates undergo ~24 months of intensive adult technical, physical, and operational training before becoming flight-eligible for International Space Station (ISS) and Artemis lunar missions.

CORE PROGRAM GOALS

Systems, EVA & Expeditionary Mastery

Trainees master ISS & Orion spacecraft systems (ECLSS, EPS, GNC), Neutral Buoyancy Laboratory (NBL) extravehicular activity in the 6.2-million-gallon pool, Canadarm2 (SSRMS) 7-DOF robotics, T-38N Talon high-performance jet crew resource management, and Russian language operations.

AUTHENTIC NASA STANDARDS

NASA-STD-3001 & Flight Rules

Every module in this simulator reflects documented NASA technical baselines: 4.3 psia (29.6 kPa) EMU operating pressure, 14.7 psia ISS cabin atmosphere, 408 km Low Earth Orbit mechanics, and Human Factors & Behavioral Performance (HFBP) expeditionary standards.

NASA Voice Loop Protocol Reference

  • Call Hierarchy: Always state "[Receiver], [Sender] on [Loop]" — e.g., "Houston, Station on Space-to-Ground 1."
  • Quindar Tones: Classic NASA 250ms intro (2,525 Hz) and outro (2,475 Hz) PTT tones keyed remote ground transmitters.
  • S-Band vs Ku/Ka-Band: S-Band carries primary command/telemetry and audio; Ku/Ka-Band handles high-rate payload data and HD video via TDRSS geostationary satellites.
